# ABC Helper Layer & Compliance Snapshot

The ABC Helper Layer is designed to streamline regulatory reviews and distributor onboarding.

## Compliance Snapshot
The "Regulator View" of a Smart Tag provides a consolidated scannable page of all legal registrations.

### Included Fields:
- **TTB COLA Number**: Direct link to the federal public registry.
- **State Registrations**: Current status and IDs for each state of distribution.
- **Control State Codes**: NABCA/Control item codes for state-run markets.
- **Compliance Documents**: Digital copies of label approvals and formulas.

## State Templates
Still includes guided fields for the following states:
- **Missouri**: Brand registration and conditional approval dates.
- **Kansas**: Annual renewal tracking and distributor franchise info.
- **Texas**: TABC product registration links.

## Disclaimer
Still Smart Tags are not a replacement for official ABC, TTB, or state registration systems. They organize producer-supplied records, links, documents, and internal asset history into one scannable digital record.
